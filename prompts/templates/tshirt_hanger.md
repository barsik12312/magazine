```

### TAG (бирка)
```

### MODEL_FRONT (на модели, перед)
```
Candid lifestyle photo of a [MODEL DESCRIPTION] wearing a [COLOR] oversized t-shirt with [PRINT DESCRIPTION] on the chest. [POSE]. [BACKGROUND]. Natural daylight, shot on iPhone 15 Pro, realistic skin texture with pores, natural hair, slight motion blur on edges. No AI artifacts. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

### MODEL_BACK (на модели, спина)
```
Candid lifestyle photo from behind of a [MODEL DESCRIPTION] wearing a [COLOR] oversized t-shirt with [BACK PRINT DESCRIPTION] on the back. [POSE]. [BACKGROUND]. Natural daylight, realistic fabric draping on body, shot on iPhone 15 Pro. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

## Переменные
- `[COLOR]` — цвет футболки
- `[PRINT DESCRIPTION]` — описание принта спереди
- `[BACK PRINT DESCRIPTION]` — описание принта сзади
- `[TAG DESIGN DESCRIPTION]` — описание бирки
- `[MODEL DESCRIPTION]` — описание модели
- `[POSE]` — поза модели
- `[BACKGROUND]` — фон для модельной съёмки
