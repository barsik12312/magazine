# Шаблон: Белье — Студийная съёмка

## Базовый промпт

### FULL_FRONT (полный рост, анфас)
```
Professional fashion photo of a [MODEL DESCRIPTION] wearing [LINGERIE DESCRIPTION]. Standing pose, facing camera, relaxed confident expression. Minimalist studio background with [BACKGROUND]. Soft key light from 45 degrees left, fill light from right, subtle rim light. Camera: 85mm equivalent, f/2.8, shallow depth of field. Realistic skin texture with natural pores, visible fabric weave on [FABRIC TYPE]. Color temperature: warm (3800K). Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

### FULL_BACK (полный рост, сзади)
```
Professional fashion photo from behind of a [MODEL DESCRIPTION] wearing [LINGERIE DESCRIPTION]. Standing pose, slight turn of head showing profile. [BACKGROUND]. Soft even lighting, emphasis on [FABRIC DETAILS]. Camera: 85mm, f/2.8. Realistic fabric texture, natural skin. Aspect ratio 4:5.
```

### DETAIL (крупный план)
```
Close-up detail shot of [SPECIFIC DETAIL] on [LINGERIE DESCRIPTION]. Macro-level texture visibility: [FABRIC TYPE] weave, stitching, elastic edge, [DECORATIVE ELEMENTS]. Soft studio lighting with slight specular highlights on [MATERIAL]. Shot at 100mm equivalent, f/4. Aspect ratio 1:1.
```

## Переменные
- `[MODEL DESCRIPTION]` — из досье модели
- `[LINGERIE DESCRIPTION]` — тип + цвет + материал
- `[BACKGROUND]` — нейтральный студийный/спальня/лофт
- `[FABRIC TYPE]` — кружево/хлопок/шёлк/сетка
- `[FABRIC DETAILS]` — детали конструкции
- `[DECORATIVE ELEMENTS]` — банты, стразы, вышивка
- `[MATERIAL]` — тип поверхности для бликов
